A nine-future quiz on advanced AI

Which AI governance future are you steering toward?

There are nine credible visions for how humanity could navigate the road to superintelligence, from today's Silicon Valley free-for-all to a single global project run jointly by rival powers. Which one you favor is not really about AI. It comes down to a handful of beliefs about timelines, power, and trust. Answer eight questions and find out where you land.

Format

8 questions. About two minutes. No right answers, just your cruxes.

Based on "Strategic Visions in AI Governance"
Oscar Delaney, Maria Kostylew, Oliver Guest, Peter Wildeford. IAPS, 2026.
