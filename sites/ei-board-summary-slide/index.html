Embodied Intelligence — Our Role & How We Engage

A worked example of how qonvolv Ventures positions on a frontier theme: aspiration, primary role, the roles that amplify the edge, and how we engage partners.

Our aspiration
Become a regional and global leader in textile & garment production automation. Vertical depth over horizontal breadth.
Primary role
🎼
Curator-owner of end-to-end solutions
Dyson model. We define the spec, commission the best parts, and own the integrated solution. We do not rebuild grippers, sensors, or foundation models.
Secondary roles · how we amplify the edge
🏭
User
Our factories are the validation environment. Real heat, humidity, line speed.
🚚
Distributor
qonvolv Innovation channel takes proven solutions to global textile customers.
💰
Investor
Capital where it deepens alignment, captures upside beyond our vertical.
🧠
Know-how contributor
40+ years of operator depth codified into specs, SOPs, and scene libraries.
📣
Promoter
Industry events, salons, papers; we set the agenda for textile EI.
How we engage partners
1
Stay open
Intersect with multiple EI partners across the horizontal stack.
2
Prioritise
Partners with strategic intent in textile that we can pitch the vertical to.
3
Start small
POC scoped to one workstation or even a single technical element.
4
Pave for JV
Selectively elevate to JV or joint lab as the pipeline of solutions matures.
Let's not
Build from zero / own every component. Sensors, chips, models — incumbents win.
Do academic-only research. No deploy intent = no commercial signal.
