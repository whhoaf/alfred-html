New Year's Eve

Fireworks in Hirafu at 20:00, then again at midnight

We found no fireworks or street countdown in Sapporo, Otaru or Hakodate for 31 Dec 2026. Every confirmed show is at a ski resort, which is why we spend New Year in Niseko. The default is both shows: Hirafu at 20:00, then Niseko Village for the 23:59 countdown.

Hirafu, then Niseko Village for the countdown

  • 17:30Dinner at the lodge (included). Ask for a seating that finishes by 19:00.
  • 19:15Drive to Hirafu, 4 min on a dry road.
  • 20:00Fireworks over the slope near ALPEN NODE. Warm up at Powderhood, open to 21:00.
  • 22:50Drive to Niseko Village: 9.3 km, 13 min usual, allow 25.
  • 23:30Taiko drums at Flame Bar, Hilton Niseko Village, to 23:50.
  • 23:59Countdown fireworks, to about 00:15.
  • 00:25Leave. Back at the lodge about 00:50. The front desk closes at 22:00, so get the late-entry code first.

Winter driving

Things to remember on the road

16:00
Arrive before dark

Sunset is about 16:05. Black ice and deer come out at dusk. One main drive a day. Two drives end in the dark (26 and 28 Dec), so go slowly.

0.0
Driver drinks nothing

Zero tolerance, including the morning after. That means New Year's Eve (the Jigokudani drive is at 08:30) and the evening of 3 Jan (the car goes back on 4 Jan). A passenger who rides with a drunk driver can be fined up to ¥500,000.

×3
Triple the gap

Ice is about ten times as slippery as dry road. Brake early and gently, worst at junctions, bridges and shade.

NOC
Check the cover

Basic cover still leaves a ¥20,000 to ¥50,000 charge if the car slides into a snowbank. Ask at pick-up whether the booking includes the NOC cover.

½
Refuel at half a tank

Country petrol stations close early and may shut 1 to 3 Jan. Fuel up before the long drives on 28 Dec and 1 Jan.

✕
Some roads are shut

The Bibai to Furano road and the Niseko Panorama Line are closed all winter, but maps still route you over them. Route 237 over Kanayama Pass and Nakayama Pass can close in storms. Check Northern Road Navi every morning.
