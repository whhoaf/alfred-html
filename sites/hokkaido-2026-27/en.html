Route

Furano first, Niseko for New Year, Sapporo at the end

One night in Sapporo to land, then up to Furano to ride, across to Otaru for sushi, three nights in Niseko over New Year, an onsen night in Jozankei on New Year's Day, and two nights in Sapporo as the city opens up again.

Winter drive times include a snow margin and no stops. The sun sets at about 16:05, so every drive after the first day is planned to finish by 15:30 to 16:00.

What this route gives up. Most of our Sapporo eating now comes after New Year, while places are still reopening: Daruma is back on 3 Jan, Shirokuma stays shut until after 3 Jan, and the big sushi counters have not posted their New Year hours yet. Furano and Otaru carry the eating before the holiday. Furano is only an afternoon and a night, and only its Furano zone is open that day. And 28 Dec is a long drive of 3 to 3.5 hours, so we leave early.

New Year's Eve

The countdowns are at the ski resorts, not in the city

We could not find any fireworks or street countdown announced in Sapporo, Otaru or Hakodate for 31 Dec 2026. Every confirmed show is at a ski resort. That is why Niseko gets New Year.

Niseko: confirmed for 2026, and the only midnight fireworks

  • 19:15Torch run down the Banzai slope, Niseko Village (to 20:00)
  • 20:00Fireworks over the slopes at Grand Hirafu, at the base of the Ace Gondola
  • LateTaiko drum show at the Hilton, Niseko Village, leading into midnight
  • 23:59Countdown fireworks at Niseko Village. Watch from in front of the Hilton side entrance.
  • 06:301 Jan: first sunrise gondola up Mt Annupuri (tickets from 06:15)
Good
  • Ride all day, fireworks at night, sunrise the next morning, all in one place.
  • Lots of bars and restaurants in Hirafu.
Watch out
  • Our pension is on the quiet Annupuri side, a short drive from the Village. The driver drinks nothing that night, or we book a taxi early.
  • The busiest week of the year. Book dinner now.

Furano: cheaper, quieter, more local

  • 23:15Kita no Daimonji at Hinode Park, Kamifurano: a 70 m by 50 m 大 character lit by about 300 torches on a snowy hillside
  • 00:00Countdown, the 大 lights up, then drums, sake barrel opening and fireworks. Over by about 00:15.
  • 00:00Shin Furano Prince Hotel did midnight fireworks, a torch run and free sparkling wine last year (2025). The 2026 plan is not out yet.
Good
  • Rooms are easy to find and cheaper. Lift tickets are ¥9,000.
  • The Blue Pond light-up in Biei is nearby.
Watch out
  • A little over half of Niseko's December snow, and a smaller food scene.
  • The organiser has not published this year's timetable yet. The tourism board lists the event.

Sapporo: a calm night, no fireworks

  • EveNo city fireworks or street countdown announced. Odori Park lights already ended on 25 Dec.
  • 00:00Hokkaido Jingu opens its gates for the first shrine visit of the year. In past years about 800,000 people came over the first three days.
  • 00:10Roughly the last subway back. After midnight you need a taxi.
Good
  • Walkable, warm, plenty of hotel rooms.
Watch out
  • Daruma, Shirokuma and the Beer Garden are all closed on 31 Dec. Many ramen shops close too.
  • No moment at midnight unless you are at the shrine.

Snowboarding

An afternoon in Furano, then two days in Niseko

Furano gets one afternoon and one night: quiet slopes and cheaper tickets. Niseko gets two full days over New Year: four linked resorts on one mountain, the most reliable December snow, and the most to do after dark.

27 Dec · afternoon and night

Furano

¥9,000 1 day, each
  • Only the Furano zone is open on 27 Dec. Kitanomine opens around 28 Dec.
  • Night riding runs to 19:30 from 26 Dec to 3 Jan. A night-only ticket is ¥3,900. Check at the desk whether the day ticket covers the night too.
  • December snow averages about 130 cm, a little over half of Niseko's.
  • Rental in town at Snow Force: board and boots ¥8,000, full set ¥12,000 a day.

¥26,500 2-day pass, each

  • The 2-day pass is for consecutive days only, so we ride 30 and 31 Dec.

Food

Eat well before 31 Dec, then again from 3 Jan

Most independent restaurants close on 31 Dec and 1 Jan, and some stay shut to 3 Jan. Furano and Otaru are our big eating days before the holiday, New Year's Day dinner is at the ryokan, and Sapporo gets a proper food day on 3 Jan.

Pick-up day, Sat 26 Dec

  1. At immigration, ask for a paper landing stamp or sticker rather than using the automatic gate. Some rental companies refuse the car if they cannot see your landing date.
  2. Walk to the rental desks on the domestic terminal 1F, ANA side, near exit 3. The 3F link between the terminals is closed for works until about June 2027, so take the lower floor and allow 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Bring passport, Macau licence, the international driving permit, the booking number, and a credit card in the driver's name. The permit must have been issued after 5 Jan 2026 to stay valid to 4 Jan. A second driver needs the same papers.
  4. Be at the desk, or phone them, within an hour of the 16:00 booking. Otherwise the booking is cancelled.
  5. Take the free shuttle to the depot, 10 to 15 minutes. Photograph every panel, the tyres, the fuel gauge and the odometer. Check the ETC card light, the ice scraper and snow brush, and set the GPS to English or Chinese.

Winter driving

Things to remember on the road

16:00
Arrive before dark

Sunset is about 16:05. Black ice and deer come out at dusk. One main drive per day.

0.0
Driver drinks nothing

Zero tolerance, including the morning after. A passenger who rides with a drunk driver can be fined up to ¥500,000.

×3
Triple the gap

Ice is about ten times as slippery as dry road. Brake early and gently, worst at junctions, bridges and shade.

NOC
Take full cover

Basic cover still leaves a ¥20,000 to ¥50,000 charge if the car slides into a snowbank. The extra cover is about ¥6,000 for the trip, and both picks above include it.

½
Refuel at half a tank

Country petrol stations close early and may shut 1 to 3 Jan.

✕
Some roads are shut

The Bibai to Furano road and the Niseko Panorama Line are closed all winter, but maps still route you over them. Nakayama Pass can close in storms. Check road conditions before every drive on Northern Road Navi.

Which Jozankei room?

Shiraito with its own onsen bath for HK$4,062, or Hatagoya, the best reviewed, for HK$2,985 with public baths only. This page assumes Shiraito.

Furano: hotel or flat?

Which Niseko bed?

Bellary for breakfast and reviews, UNO for price, or Izumikyo for a private bathroom near Hirafu at HK$338 more than Bellary. This page assumes Bellary.

Bring our own boards and outerwear?
